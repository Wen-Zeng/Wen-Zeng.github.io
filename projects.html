My Projects

Project 1 · Research database

A Comprehensive GIS Database for China’s Land Surface Transport Network

An open-access GIS database tracing the development of China’s motorways, national highways, high-speed railways, regular railways and waterways from 1993 to 2020.

Developed with Steven J. Davis and Meijun Qian, the project supports research on transport access, network connectivity and socioeconomic development.

Project 2 · Working paper

A Comprehensive GIS Database for China’s Surface Transport Network with Implications for Transport and Socioeconomics Research

A working paper with Steven J. Davis and Meijun Qian examining the expansion of China’s transport network, changes in accessibility and connectivity, and the importance of measuring access through actual transport entry points.
