Project Overview

We assemble a detailed GIS database covering national highways, modern motorways, traditional railways, high-speed railways and waterways. The accompanying paper describes its construction and examines changes in China’s transport network over 1993–2020.

The interactive map provides a way to explore selected transport layers and years. For the research database and supporting documentation, see the resources below.

Research Highlights

A rapidly expanding network

Total network length grew to more than three times its 1993 level. Motorways and high-speed rail accounted for roughly half of the increase.

Closer access to transport

In the February 2025 paper, average distance from county centroids to transport access points fell from 13.28 km to 5.81 km between 1993 and 2020.

Stronger network connections

The same version reports increases of 22% in county-level connectivity and 115% in centrality.

Actual access matters

Distance to motorway entrances can differ substantially from straight-line distance to routes, with implications for studies of infrastructure’s economic effects.

Source: February 2025 working paper (BFI PDF). Figures refer to that version.
