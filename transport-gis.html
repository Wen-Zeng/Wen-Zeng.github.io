A Comprehensive GIS Database of China's Land Surface Transport Network

We have developed a comprehensive GIS database of China's land surface transport network. This includes data on high-speed railways (2003–2020), traditional railways (1995–2020), modern motorways (1993–2020), national highways (1993, 2013–2020), and waterways.

The China Land Surface Transport System Database is freely accessible. Users of the database should cite the following source:

Davis, Steven J., Meijun Qian, and Wen Zeng (2025). "A Comprehensive GIS Dataset for China's Surface Transport System with Implications for Transport and Socionomics Research," working paper, Stanford University.

Technical details of the dataset are available here: Technical Documentation – February 2025. You can download the data freely from Steven Davis' Website. Search for 'GIS Database' on the page to locate the download link.

For more information, please contact: wen.zeng.001@gmail.com or meijunqian@gmail.com

If the transport layers doesn’t load, please refresh the webpage and try again.
